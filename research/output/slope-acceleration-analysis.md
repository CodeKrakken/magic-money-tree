## Interpretation notes

- An isolated high-return combination is not sufficient evidence of a useful signal.
- Broadly positive neighbouring parameter values are more informative than a single extreme result.
- Very low trade counts can produce unstable returns and profit factors.
- The combined Part 3 results aggregate the six historical months; they are not a continuous month-by-month equity curve.
- Current Binance-market selection still has the survivorship bias documented by the research pipeline.
- This analysis is therefore intended to identify hypotheses for further out-of-sample testing, not to establish a validated trading strategy.
